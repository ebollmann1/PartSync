EbizCharge Functionality Overview

The purpose of this document is to provide a high-level overview of the features and functionalities available in our Odoo integration.



EBizCharge Features Within Odoo

All native Odoo Enterprise features are accessible through the dashboard by selecting the grid menu in the upper left corner.

Customers

  • Ability to switch between EBizCharge merchant accounts and Odoo companies on a customer profile.

Invoices

  • Ability to pay with EBizCharge using a new or saved credit card, bank account, or an EMV device.
  • Ability to generate EBizCharge payment links on an individual invoice.
  • Ability to upload an individual invoice to the EBizCharge Hub.
  • Ability to send an email pay request to a customer when viewing an invoice.
  • Ability to view, export, print and download an EBizCharge invoice.

Quotations and Sales Orders

  • Ability to select a transaction type (pre auth or deposit) and register payment for a specified amount.
  • Ability to upload an individual quotation to the EBizCharge Hub.
  • Ability to generate EBizCharge payment links on an individual sales order.
  • Ability to register a sales order payment with EBizCharge using a new or saved credit card, bank account, or an EMV device for deposits, and credit card and EMV for pre auths.

EBizCharge Module Features

Transaction History

  • Ability to void or refund past transactions and email a receipt of a transaction.
  • Ability to switch between EBizCharge merchant accounts when viewing transaction history.
  • Ability to export list of transactions as an .xls file.

Batch Processing for Invoices

  • Ability to view a list of open and processed invoices.
  • Ability to process one, multiple, or all open invoices.
  • Ability to email receipts to customers.

Email Pay for Invoices

  • Ability to send email pay requests for open invoices.
  • Ability to select a preexisting email template and edit the requested payment amount.
  • Ability to view, remove, and resend pending payment requests.
  • Ability to apply one or more received payments to a respective order.

Request Payment Methods

  • Ability to request a payment method via email.
  • Ability to select payment method type, premade email template, and add additional email comments when sending a payment request.
  • Ability to view pending requests and resend requests.
  • Ability to remove a customer’s payment method.

Generate Payment Links

  • Ability to generate payment links for sales orders and invoices.
  • Ability to enter a custom request amount when generating a payment link.
  • Ability to select a template to appear on the EBizCharge payment webform.
  • Ability to cancel payment requests by removing the payment link.
  • Ability to copy and paste payment link and preview EBizCharge payment webform.
  • Ability to make a payment with EBizCharge from a native Odoo payment link.

Sync Upload

  • Ability to auto or manual sync customers, invoices, sales orders, products, or credit notes to the EBizCharge Hub.
  • Ability to export as an .xls file.

Sync Download

  • Ability to download payments from the EBizCharge Hub into Odoo.
  • Ability to import received payments into Odoo from the EBizCharge Hub.

Setup

  • Ability to view list of email and receipt templates and export templates as an .xlsx or .csv file.

EBizCharge Settings

Scheduler Settings

  • Ability to configure scheduled actions by interval, frequency, and next execution date.
  • Ability to activate or deactivate the scheduler.

EBizCharge Merchant Account Setup

  • Ability to enable automatic upload of customers, invoices, sales orders, products, and credit notes to the EBizCharge Hub.
  • Ability to set automatic document download frequency.
  • Ability to enable auto download and apply payments.
  • Ability to select default transaction type (pre auth or deposit) for sales order payments, email pay requests, and generated payment links for sales orders.
  • Ability to enable automatic payment link generation when invoices and sales orders are created.
  • Ability to manage merchant accounts, set default merchant account, and assign multiple companies to a merchant account.
  • Ability to enable EMV to process transactions, set a default EMV device, and configure, add, and edit EMV device settings.
  • Ability to configure a website.

Help & Contact Us

  • Ability to view the support team’s contact information.
  • Ability to access EBizCharge for Odoo CRM, EBizCharge for Odoo eCommerce, and EBizCharge Knowledge Base.
  • Ability to provide feedback on EBizCharge for Odoo.
